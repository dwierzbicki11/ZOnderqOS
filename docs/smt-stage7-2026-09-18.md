# SMT Stage 7 — repeated fixed IPI rendezvous

Base main: `bcdccc5dbcbcfec18d1877efb34646d6dc3f5db6` after the Stage 6 merge.

Stage 7 adds a narrow native-only proof after Stage 6. Each AP has already booted, published READY, completed the native Stage 5 work counter, and acknowledged the initial Stage 6 fixed IPI. Stage 7 then sends three more fixed IPIs to every AP and requires the per-CPU IPI counter to reach exactly four total acknowledgements.

This proves a repeated AP wake/acknowledge/return-to-park path through the native IDT and LAPIC EOI. It does not run managed code, the scheduler, GC, or general interrupt dispatch on APs.
